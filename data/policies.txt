BrewBean Coffee Co. - Customer Policies

Refund Policy:
If a customer is not satisfied with a drink, we will remake it free of charge within 15 minutes of purchase.
Online orders of coffee beans can be returned within 7 days if the bag is unopened. Refunds are processed
to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Delivery Policy:
We deliver coffee beans across Pakistan. Delivery within Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad takes 1 to 2 days.
Other cities take 3 to 5 business days. Delivery is free on orders above PKR 5,000; otherwise a PKR 250 fee applies.

Opening Hours:
All cafes are open from 8 AM to 11 PM, Monday to Sunday. On public holidays, cafes open at 10 AM.

Wi-Fi Policy:
Free Wi-Fi is available in all cafes. The password is printed on every receipt and changes weekly.
