BrewBean Coffee Co. - Company Overview

BrewBean Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee company founded in 2018 in Lahore, Pakistan by Sara Malik and Usman Raza.
The company operates 12 cafes across Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, and also sells roasted coffee beans online.

Mission: To serve ethically sourced, freshly roasted coffee and create warm community spaces.

Headquarters: 45-B Gulberg III, Lahore, Pakistan.
Employees: Approximately 180 staff members.
Customer support email: support@brewbean.example

Our coffee beans are sourced from Ethiopia, Colombia and Brazil. All beans are roasted in-house at our Lahore roastery
every Tuesday and Friday to guarantee freshness.
