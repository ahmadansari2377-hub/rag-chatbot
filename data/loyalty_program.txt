BrewBean Coffee Co. - BeanPoints Loyalty Program

BeanPoints is our free loyalty program. Customers can join through the BrewBean mobile app.

How it works:
- Earn 1 BeanPoint for every PKR 100 spent.
- 100 BeanPoints can be redeemed for one free drink of any size.
- Members get a free birthday drink every year.
- Gold tier: customers who earn 1,000 BeanPoints in a calendar year become Gold members and get 15% off all orders
  plus early access to new seasonal drinks.

BeanPoints expire 12 months after they are earned. Points cannot be transferred between accounts.
