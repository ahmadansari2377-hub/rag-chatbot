BrewBean Coffee Co. - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you offer vegan options?
A: Yes. All our drinks can be made with oat or almond milk, and our Mango Smoothie is vegan by default.

Q: Can I book a cafe for a private event?
A: Yes. Our Gulberg (Lahore) and Clifton (Karachi) branches can be booked for private events of up to 40 guests.
Email events@brewbean.example at least 5 days in advance.

Q: Do you sell gift cards?
A: Yes, gift cards are available in PKR 2,000, PKR 5,000 and PKR 10,000 values, in-store and on the app.

Q: Are you hiring?
A: We regularly hire baristas and cafe managers. Send your CV to careers@brewbean.example.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Free parking is available at all branches except our Blue Area (Islamabad) branch.
